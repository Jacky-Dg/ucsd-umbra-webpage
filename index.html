ABOUT US

The purpose of creating this organization here at UCSD is to open the first outside chapter of the Undergraduate Missiles, Ballistics, and Rocketry Association (UMBRA) that has originated at California State Polytechnic University Pomona. UMBRA at UCSD provides students with hands-on engineering opportunities that align with their respective coursework in order for them to develop their skills and expertise. Additionally, UMBRA offers a wide variety of opportunities for members that include general meetings that keep members informed and recruit new prospective students. UMBRA will offer resume building, interview practice as well as more in depth training to hone skills such as CAD, engineering drawings, manufacturing, and internship panels. To support technical growth, UMBRA will implement internal training to establish skills in order to compete in FAR competitions. Overall, UMBRA aims to provide real-world, hands-on engineering experience to students and expand their network and skill set necessary for them to be as best prepared to acquire internships or full time jobs once they graduate.
